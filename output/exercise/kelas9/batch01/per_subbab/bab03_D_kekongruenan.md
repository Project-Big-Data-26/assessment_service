- **A. $70^\circ$** ✅

- Karena $\Delta ABE \cong \Delta ADF$, maka $\text{Luas}(\Delta ABE) = \text{Luas}(\Delta ADF)$.

### 45. [hard] kelas9-b03-d-hard-bt01-015
